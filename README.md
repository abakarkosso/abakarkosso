### How I build

Most AI demos tell you what the model said. I try to also tell you how often it's right.
Internship Radar won't hide a posting until a rule has been at least 90% accurate on postings I labelled
by hand and never tuned against. Economics taught me to ask "compared to what?", and I bring that
question to every model I ship.

### What's next

**`rulekeeper`** (in progress). A recent paper found that Claude Code's context compaction kept 53% of
safety rules after one round and 10% after five. I'm testing that on real `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md`
files from public repos, using survival analysis from econometrics to see which kinds of rules get
forgotten first. The findings and the tool will both be open source.
